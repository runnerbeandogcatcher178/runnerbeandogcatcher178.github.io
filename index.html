🤔 What is snap?

snap is a local, deterministic decision engine that takes messy, unstructured input and produces clean, typed decisions instantly. Unlike traditional AI tools that generate text, snap performs one forward pass and gives you structured, reliable output with zero generated text.

Think of it as a lightning-fast, always-working translator between chaos and clarity. Whether you're dealing with messy data, unclear user input, or complex state that needs categorization, snap makes the decision for you - every time, the same way, with complete certainty.

Key benefit: snap runs entirely on your computer. Your data never leaves your machine. Your decisions are instant and always repeatable.
